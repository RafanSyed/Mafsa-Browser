# Masfa Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [10/2/2026]

Masfa ("the app") is a web browser for iPhone that checks websites and searches before they load. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

---

## The short version

- Masfa has no accounts. You don't sign up, and I don't collect your name, email, phone number, or location.
- To decide whether a site or search should be blocked, the app sends the **domain, address, or search text** you're about to visit to Masfa's classification service.
- That data is used **only to make the block-or-allow decision**. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to track you across other apps or websites.
- Your favorites and lockdown timer stay on your device.

---

## What the app collects

### Browsing history (domains and addresses)
When you open a website, Masfa sends its domain, and in some cases the page address, to the classification service so it can be checked. The service stores a **list of domains and their verdicts** (for example, allowed or blocked) so the same site doesn't need to be re-checked each time. Domains you choose to block with the shield button are added to this list.

### Search history
When you search, Masfa sends the search text to the classification service to check it. Search text is never saved in any database.

### YouTube links
When you open a YouTube video, Masfa sends the video link to the service so the video can be checked. The youtube video is never saved in any database.

### What is not collected
Masfa does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, payment information, or health data. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting tools. 

---

## How the information is used

The information is used only for **app functionality**: deciding whether to allow or block a site, search, or video, and remembering that decision. It is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking, and it is not linked to your identity.

---

## Information stored on your device

These stay on your iPhone and are not sent to the service:

- Your favorites list
- Your lockdown timer
- Open tabs and normal browser state managed by Apple's WebKit

Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

---

## Third parties

- **Hosting provider.** The classification service runs on [Render]. Requests to the service pass through their infrastructure, and the provider may keep standard server logs for a limited time. Your IP address is not saved anywhere.
- **AI classification provider.** To classify sites, searches, and video titles, the service sends the relevant text to OPENAI api. 
- **Favicons.** The icons on the start page are loaded from Google's favicon service, which receives the domain of each favorite site so it can return its icon.
- **Websites you visit.** Once a page is allowed to load, the website you visit receives your request like it would in any browser and has its own privacy practices. This policy doesn't cover them.

I don't sell your information and don't share it with data brokers or advertisers.

## Children

Masfa is not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. It has no accounts or social features.

## Security

Requests to the classification service are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No system is perfectly secure, and Masfa is a filtering aid, not a guarantee. Filtering can make mistakes in both directions.
